Where each licence text in this folder was fetched from (the runtime it belongs to is AppImage/type2-runtime, tag 20251108,
commit dd6cebedcbddde9c82f89b011e8e1d40b6e43868). Its LICENSE lists musl, libfuse, squashfuse, libzstd and zlib; its
src/runtime/Makefile also links mimalloc. Versions follow type2-runtime's scripts where they name one (libfuse 3.15.0,
squashfuse 0.5.2); musl, zstd, zlib and mimalloc come from Alpine's packages at build time, so those texts are from a recent upstream
tag and the licence terms (not the version) are what they record.
