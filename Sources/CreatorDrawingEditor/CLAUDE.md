# CreatorDrawingEditor

Notes for this module, moved from the root `CLAUDE.md` (which Claude Code loads in every session) so they load only when work touches this folder. The project-wide rules, commands and toolchain notes stay in the root file.

- `CreatorDrawingEditor`: the drawing editor. `@MainActor @Observable DrawingEditorModel` holds the open sheet, tool, selection, hover and canvas transform and turns gestures into `DrawingCommit`s (DR-2: the annotation tools, picking, selection, `remembered` last values and re-attaching) (the whole drawing document, as a `SketchCommit` carries the whole sketch) through `events.committed`, which the app stores with one `.setDrawings` step; MetalUI views in `Views/` (`SheetCanvas`, `SheetViewLayer` and `SheetFrameLayer` `.equatable()`, `SheetTextView`, `SheetTabs`, `DrawingToolbar`, `DrawingInspector`; `Templates/` holds `DrawingTemplateFolder` and `DrawingTemplateStore`; `DrawingClipboard` is the in-memory clipboard of ⌘C, ⌘X and ⌘V). Depends on CreatorDrawing, CreatorKernel, CreatorGeometry, CreatorStyle and MetalUI only, never CreatorGraph. Tests: `swift test --filter CreatorDrawingEditorTests`.

Drawings speed (linework memo): `DrawingEditorModel.linework` is a `LineworkMemo` passed to `SheetComposer.compose` in `rebuildPicture`, so a drag of an annotation re-maps no view; `lineworkMapped` is a running total of views mapped, read by `DragWorkTests`.

Drawings speed (layer buckets): `rebuildPicture` builds `DrawingEditorModel.layers` (`SheetLayers`) from the composed picture, passing the previous layers. Buckets: `frame` (no view), and per view `linework` (the view's strokes with no annotation) and `annotations` (annotation strokes, and every fill and text of the view, so the old order of strokes, then fills, then texts holds). A bucket keeps its previous `revision` when its arrays are `==` and takes the next otherwise, so a layer's `==` compares the revision, not arrays: `SheetFrameLayer`, `SheetViewLayer` (linework) and `SheetAnnotationLayer` (annotations, with the hovered line, the selection outline and the pending fade, drawn over the linework in the old z order). A bucket also carries its `SheetLayers` lineage (a fresh UUID whenever the layers are built from nothing: a new editor model or `layers = nil`), which a layer's `==` compares with the revision, since revisions restart at 1 in a new lineage. While a view is pending its linework and its annotations fade separately at 0.4 each, so strokes that overlap across the two layers compound slightly (accepted). A pan filters nothing; a dimension drag evaluates only its view's `SheetAnnotationLayer` and the `SheetSelectionLayer`, which takes the selected annotations and marks from `SheetLayers.selection(annotations:marks:)`. `picture.strokes` keeps its flat order: `hoveredStroke` and `hoverCandidate` index into it, so the hover lookups stay on the flat picture. A zoom step still evaluates every layer, but upright text is laid out at the octave-band reference zoom (`SheetTextZoom.reference(for:)`, `2^round(log2(zoom))`, in `Views/SheetTextZoom.swift`) in an `.equatable()` `SheetUprightTexts` and drawn under `scaleEffect(zoom / reference, anchor: .topLeading)` from `SheetTexts` (zoom-text plan R1 to R3, R5): a zoom step inside one band re-evaluates no text and MetalUI re-rasterises it on its 2^(1/8) ladder, and crossing a band rebuilds each layer's upright text once. Only text with an upright angle whose em at the true zoom stays within MetalUI's 256 px re-key cap (`SheetTextZoom.isScaled`: em times 2 for backing scale 2, R7; the cap is `TextZoom.maximumEmPixels`, past it MetalUI upscales and the text goes soft, gap DZ-d) goes in the scaled group, and its copy is drawn with angle 0 so a 2π angle gives MetalUI no rotation to resample (R8). Rotated text (angle not a whole turn, R2), text past the cap (3.5 mm past zoom 26.3, 7 mm past zoom 13.1) and strokes still rebuild per zoom step; upright text paints before rotated text within a layer (R3). A test that counts evaluations counts one `SheetUprightTexts` per layer that has upright text. The draft picture is small and stays filtered.
