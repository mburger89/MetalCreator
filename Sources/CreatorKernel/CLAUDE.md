# CreatorKernel

Notes for this module, moved from the root `CLAUDE.md` (which Claude Code loads in every session) so they load only when work touches this folder. The project-wide rules, commands and toolchain notes stay in the root file.

- `CreatorKernel`: the `Kernel` protocol (DR-3: `cutAway(_:by:tag:)` with `SectionCutter`, `Section/`), `Solid`, tagged topology tables, `FakeKernel` for tests (it refuses extruded holes that lie outside
  the outline's bounds, as OCCT refuses strays). `Kernel.readModel(_:format:tag:)` (STEP or IGES bytes to a solid, faces `.imported(face: i)`), `Kernel.scaled(_:by:tag:)` and `Kernel.compound(_:tag:)` refuse by default and in `FakeKernel`; OCCT answers them (KiCad import KI-2). `Kernel.place(_:at:qualifying:tag:)` makes every placed copy of a pattern in one
  call: copy `i` is a tool moved by a `Transform`, its tags requalified (`Topology.qualified(item:_:)`: node → `qualify(node)`,
  item → `i`, nested blend source edges too). A pattern over a tree renames each copy afterwards
  (`Topology.withInstanceItem(_:)`,
  no kernel call): `InstancePath` is the one place a path becomes a tag item and back, `{i}` being the item `i` (so a
  pick saved
  before trees resolves unchanged) and `{a;b}` to `{a;b;c;d}` (indices up to 32,767) one negative `Int`.
  `NodeID.isInstanceQualified` (a version-8 UUID) tells those nodes from any other,
  and `Topology.vanishedInstances(in:)` finds the copies a pick names that a topology no longer has.
  `Kernel.distances(from:to:signed:)` measures a batch of points to a `DistanceTarget` (`.solid`, `.faces`, `.edges`; fields
  spec §3.1): millimetres to the surface, negative inside a solid when signed (the sign is ignored for faces and edges);
  `DistanceTarget.validate()` gives every kernel the same refusals and `.identity` is what the field cache keys on. `FakeKernel`
  is exact for its boxes, for planar faces measured to their infinite plane and for straight or circular edges
  (`EdgeCurve.distance(from:)`, shared with Cull by Region), and throws `unsupported` for the rest.
  `Kernel.tessellate(_:tolerance:angle:)` takes the angular deflection too (`DisplayAngle.standard` 0.5 rad, `.zebra` 0.05; `ViewportModel.displayAngle` picks one, and zebra stripes need the finer mesh to read a fillet smoothly). `Kernel.faceParametrization(of:in:)` and `Kernel.faceCoordinates(of:on:in:)` (implicit spec §3.5, Face mapping) say how a face is parametrized
  (`FaceParametrization`: the middle of u and v, millimetres per unit, the period of a coordinate in which the face closes on itself, a degenerate
  edge) and where points stand against it (`FaceSample`: the nearest point's u and v, the signed distance along the outward normal); the default
  refuses, `FakeKernel` answers for planar faces, `OCCTKernel` for any (`occt_face_chart_info_of`, `occt_face_coordinates`, `cocct_face_chart.cpp`).
  `Kernel.fillet(_:edges:radius:profile:tag:)` takes a `FilletProfile` (`.circular`, or `.curvatureContinuous(weight:)`, the weight scaling the section's end tangents against the
  circle's, validated by `validate()`) and gives a `FilletResult` (the solid, `tangentOnlyCorners` and `tangentOnlyJoins`, the counts the Fillet node warns about); `KernelError.notCurvatureContinuous(radius:largest:)` is the strip refusal, naming the largest radius
  that works (`OCCTKernel.largestValidBlend`, searched with the G2 profile). OCCT builds the circular fillet, then each strip is replaced by a skinned quintic G2 section surface (`occt_fillet_g2`, `cocct_g2_blend.cpp`, with the phases in `cocct_g2_strip.cpp`, `cocct_g2_ends.cpp`, `cocct_g2_corner.cpp` and `cocct_g2_seam.cpp` over `cocct_g2_blend_internal.hpp`; sections adaptive, 9 to 129,
  each a quintic Bezier matching position, tangent and normal curvature of both neighbours) and the seams are measured on the final solid (position 1e-4 mm, angle 0.01 degrees, curvature within 1 %; evenly and with three samples in every span between sections too narrow for the even ones, `span_samples`, on the candidate as well, since a piece of two sections at a strip's end is only a chord), which `BRepCheck_Analyzer` then checks:
  an invalid solid is never returned. Covered: closed strips (a hole's edge), chains into walls, slanted and curved run-outs, mitres (two strips meeting in a crease, checked for position only) and kinked joins (the strips share
  one end section; counted and warned about for the join itself, the seams beside it held to the whole promise up to the join). Corners: a degree-17 triangular Bezier G2 patch on three-fillet corners at any angle (accepted only when its seams, regularity and curvature pass), otherwise
  a counted G1 refill. The patch's sides are mitred (spec erratum "mitred corners"): G2 to both strips at a corner of the hole needs each side to leave it along the other strip's spring
  (else the two strips fix the patch's cubic form at different values, which no polynomial patch can meet), so off a right angle each side is the curve u = u_end + g(t) on its strip, a quintic
  fitted through six samples and refused (G1 refill) when it misses by more than 1e-7 mm; where it runs into the hole the strip's B-spline is carried on exactly in place (`extend_in_u` in
  `cocct_g2_corner.cpp`: the end span re-blossomed over a longer u range, the old range's parameters unchanged), so no pcurve leaves its surface (`occt_pcurve_domain_excursion`, test only). The G1 refill is `BRepFill_Filling` at G1, one plain attempt (the circular corner as initial surface never worked and cost 25 s on a pyramid's apex); a corner that misses G1 fails. The history is renumbered against the final solid (a
  record whose face can't be found refuses, never dropped). The Fillet node's `continuity` (0 Tangent G1, 1 Curvature G2) and `tangency weight` (0.1 to 2, shown for G2 only); warnings "N fillet corners are tangent only (G1)." and "N fillet joins are only approximately tangent where the edges kink." (singular "1 fillet corner is …"); refusals "The fillet can't be made curvature continuous at this radius." (with the largest radius), "The fillet's corners can't be made smooth at this radius." and
  "The tangency weight is too large for this fillet." (a weight of 2 on a right angle), besides "The tangency weight must be greater than 0.".
  `FaceInfo.radius` and `axisPoint` give a cylinder's radius and a point on its axis. `Kernel.shortestDistance(between:and:)` gives a `ShapeDistance`
  (the distance and the witness points) between two picks, over `cocct_measure.cpp`'s `BRepExtrema_DistShapeShape`; `FakeKernel` is exact for points and line edges.
  `Kernel.continuity(along:of:)` grades the join along an edge between two faces as an `EdgeContinuity` (`positionGap`, `angleGap`, relative `curvatureGap`, `absoluteCurvatureGap`, `grade`: `ContinuityClass` G0 / G1 / G2) over 32 samples, against position 1e-4 mm, angle 0.01° and curvature 1 % of the larger value or 1e-4 /mm, whichever is larger; OCCT does it in `cocct_continuity.cpp` computing the second fundamental form by hand from `BRepAdaptor_Surface::D2` at the pcurve's points (normals oriented by face orientation, curvature the normal curvature across the edge), `FakeKernel` for box edges, and an edge that does not join two faces throws `.unsupported`.
  `Kernel.continuity(of:in:against:of:)` grades a sheet's free edge against a face of a second body (NS-2); `Kernel.networkSurface(u:v:tolerance:tag:)` makes the Gordon surface of crossing curves and `Kernel.boundaryPatch(_:interior:interior:continuity:tag:)` fills a loop of pieces at G0, G1 or G2 (`PatchContinuity`); `Kernel.sweep(_:along:options:guide:tag:)` takes a `SweepGuide` and `SweepOptions.twist`.
  `Kernel.loft(_:ruled:part:start:end:tag:)` (the old signature forwards with nils) takes a `LoftEnd` per end (`face`, a `LoftContinuity` of `.free` / `.tangent` / `.curvature`, `weight` 0.1 to 2; `CreatorKernel/LoftEnd.swift`, with the `KernelError` statics and `Kernel.validateLoftEnds` every kernel shares) for a picked flat face of `part`: that face's outer loop is the section at that end (side tag k is the face edge paired with profile segment k; a one-edge closed loop starts at the point nearest the paired section's start; two face ends with no profile are a bridge). Refusals: "Straight sides can't have end conditions." (ruled with any face end), "The loft's end face must be flat.", "The tangency weight is too large for this loft." (above about 2.5), a face that vanished, and a face end with no part. `FakeKernel` validates then refuses a face end with `.unsupported("loft end conditions")`; `OCCTKernel` builds it with `cocct_loft_ends.cpp` (the end-constrained skinning: every pole row our own cubic or quintic spline along the segment, periodic on a closed loop; columns adaptive from 5 per segment, 9 on a closed loop, up to 129: straight walls take 5, a full circle 17, the rounded rectangle's smooth vertices 45 and 38) over `cocct_profile.hpp` (the loop and section pieces the shim shares), and a loft with no face end is today's `ThruSections` unchanged.
  Curves and sheets (NS-1): `Curve` (`CurvePiece` lines, arcs and `BSplineCurve3D`), `BoundaryRole` (start, end, rail, piece, imported), `Solid.isClosed` (edge-level) and `Solid.hasOpenShell` (face-level: a face whose shell is not inside a solid is on an open shell; refusals, facts and export use it), `Kernel.readShapes`, and `export(_:curves:)`; a sheet's boundary edges report `faces == [f]`.

Every fillet and chamfer result is checked with OCCT's `BRepCheck_Analyzer` (`OCCTShape.isValid`) and never returned when
the check rejects it: the blend fails naming the largest size that works (`OCCTKernel.largestValidBlend`, spec Errata
(Kernel: invalid blends)). A blend OCCT can't build names one too, and a checker that itself throws
(`OCCTValidity.unchecked`) gives the generic message with no search (Errata (Kernel: largest size for blends OCCT can't
build)); a refused blend costs tens of milliseconds more than one that works (`BlendRefusalBench`,
`docs/verification/performance.md`).
